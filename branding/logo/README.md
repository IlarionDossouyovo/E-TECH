## Design System

### Concept
Minimalist "E" letterform with:
- **Negative space** technology circuit elements
- **Hexagonal frame** representing global connectivity  
- **Three accent dots** (cyan/purple) for tech nodes
- **Clean typography** with electric blue accent

### Awards-Quality Attributes
- ✅ Vector scalable (any size)
- ✅ Transparent background (main version)
- ✅ Minimalist aesthetic
- ✅ Strong negative space usage
- ✅ Premium corporate feel
- ✅ Fortune 500 quality
- ✅ Behance/Dribbble ready

## Usage Guidelines

### Do's
✅ Use transparent version for websites
✅ Use dark version on dark backgrounds
✅ Use light version on white backgrounds
✅ Maintain clear space around logo

### Don'ts
❌ Don't stretch or distort
❌ Don't change colors
❌ Don't add effects
❌ Don't rotate
